# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces professional, inclusive, and focused on evidence. Be respectful,
welcome technically grounded disagreement, distinguish people from proposals, and give credit for contributions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information
are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove violating content and restrict participation proportionately.

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization maintainers. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent
practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and recused.
