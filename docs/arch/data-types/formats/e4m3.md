## Purpose and scope

E4M3 is an assigned PTO numeric format. This page helps a reader connect its carrier layout, finite-value decomposition, and value classification; the exact contract remains in `E4M3NumericFormatDescriptor`, `E4M3FiniteDecomposition`, and `ClassifyE4M3` in the ASL owner.

<!-- PTO-READER-BLOCK: arch-format-e4m3-concepts role=concepts-state -->
## Carrier and fields

The descriptor uses a `8`-bit carrier, a `8`-bit logical lane, and `1` lane(s) per carrier. The lane has one sign bit at position `7`, `4` exponent bit(s), `3` fraction bit(s), and exponent bias `7`.

No carrier bits are constrained outside the sign, exponent, and fraction fields.

<!-- PTO-READER-BLOCK: arch-format-e4m3-rules role=rules-interactions -->
## Decomposition and classification

`E4M3FiniteDecomposition` reports whether a finite decomposition is available and, when available, returns the sign, an integer significand, and a binary exponent. `ClassifyE4M3` separately assigns the raw lane to zero, subnormal, normal, infinity, or NaN classes supported by this format.

It has signed zero, subnormals, and a quiet-NaN class, but no infinity or signaling-NaN class. Only an all-one exponent together with an all-one fraction is non-finite.

<!-- PTO-READER-BLOCK: arch-format-e4m3-boundaries role=boundaries -->
## Boundaries and exact encodings

`0x00` and `0x80` are positive and negative zero; `0x7f` is the canonical quiet NaN.

The finite-decomposition function reports availability with its returned tuple; value classification is a separate function result.
